ABOUT EVAGO

Welcome to EvaGo Geoconsulting Ltd.

EvaGo Geoconsulting was founded in 2020 to close the gap between subsurface data and the decisions that depend on it — where to drill, how deep and where to explore, and at what cost. We work across Kenya and the wider East African region, pairing hands-on fieldwork with rigorous geochemical and geophysical modelling.

Our team moves between two worlds that are usually kept separate: groundwater and minerals. Both start with the same question — what is beneath the surface — and both demand the same discipline in survey design, data interpretation, and reporting that clients can act on.

At EvaGo, we are true sustainability consultants, combining deep expertise with practical delivery.

HOW WE WORK

Our approach

01 — WATER

Hydrogeological & Borehole Siting Surveys, Borehole Drilling

Siting to production

Groundwater potential mapping

Desk and field assessment of an area's groundwater prospects ahead of any drilling commitment.

Geophysical borehole siting (ERT / VES)

Electrical resistivity tomography and vertical electrical sounding to locate the most viable borehole drill point.

Hydrogeological assessments

Aquifer characterisation, recharge and yield sustainability studies for domestic, agricultural and industrial supply.

Borehole drilling & Supervision

Borehole drilling services as well as independent oversight of drilling contractors, casing design and construction to protect the client's investment.

Pump testing & Yield analysis

Step-drawdown and constant-rate testing to confirm sustainable abstraction rates before handover.

Water quality sampling & EIA support

Physicochemical sampling and documentation to support NEMA environmental impact assessment requirements.

Environmental Impact assesment studies for NEMA certification on projects

02 — MINERALS

Mineral Exploration

Reconnaissance to drill target

Geological mapping & reconnaissance

Field mapping of lithology, structure and alteration to define mineral target areas ahead of ground survey.

Geophysical surveys

Resistivity, induced polarisation and magnetic surveys to resolve anomalies associated with mineralisation of e.g, gold, copper and beryllium.

Geochemical sampling & assay coordination

Soil, stream-sediment and rock-chip sampling programmes coordinated with certified assay laboratories to determine mineral concentrations.

Drill-target generation

Integration of geological, geophysical and geochemical datasets into ranked, defensible drill targets or mining targets.

Resource estimation support

Data compilation and QA/QC support feeding into competent-person resource estimation workflows.

Exploration reporting & compliance

Technical reporting for permitting, joint-venture partners and regulatory submission.
